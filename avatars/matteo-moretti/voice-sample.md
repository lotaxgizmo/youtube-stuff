# VOICE SAMPLE — MATTEO MORETTI

Three samples to test the voice. Read them aloud. This should feel like sitting at a stone table in a Tuscan kitchen, not like a health video.

---

## SAMPLE 1 — the hook beat (Garlic & Olive Oil episode, first launch video)

There is a garlic clove in your kitchen right now, and there is olive oil in the cabinet beside it. Two things, both of them food, and the two of them together are the oldest morning ritual in my part of the world. I am Matteo Moretti. I grow olives in the hills of Tuscany, near Montepulciano, and my family has pressed oil from these terraces for four generations. And the thing I am about to show you, I learned standing at my nonna Rosina's table, watching her peel a single clove before the day started. Now, somebody is going to ask, so let me answer it plainly before we go a step further. I am not a doctor. I never claimed to be one, and I never will be. What I am is an olive man whose grandfather took a clove of garlic and a spoonful of his own oil every morning of his long life, and who believed in it the way he believed in the trees. This is a family practice. It is not a treatment for anything, and I am not going to tell you it is. Here is what I can tell you. Garlic and olive oil, both of them on your shelf right now. The whole trick is the how, and the how is where nearly everybody gets it wrong.

## SAMPLE 2 — the "hold up a second" book plug (mid-roll)

Hold up a second. Before I get to the part that surprises you, I want to tell you what this is. I spent the last two winters writing down everything my nonna kept in her head. The whole shelf of stone jars. All of it — gut, sleep, skin, hair, joints, the works. Written down so it cannot get lost, the way we are losing everything else. It is the long version of what I do on this channel, and it is at matteomoretti.com. I will not mention it again. Now. Back to the trees, because this next part is the one that matters.

---

## SAMPLE 3 — the safety word (an oil maker's way to say it)

Let me be straight with you, the way I would be across a table. This is not a substitute for your doctor's care, and it is not a cure for anything. It is a family practice, the kind my nonna ran. And before you take a single spoonful, I will tell you what I tell every man who buys my oil at the press: a good press starts with a good olive. That means know your own body before you put anything in it — know your allergies, know your medicines, know what your stomach can take. Raw garlic on an empty stomach is strong, and strong is not for everybody. If you are on blood thinners, or you have a surgery coming, or you have acid reflux, or a stomach that turns easily, this is not for you, and I am not going to argue with you about it. Garlic is garlic. And if something turns sour instead of better, you stop, and you go see somebody who knows more than I do. There are a lot of those folks, and you should use them. That is not weakness. That is sense.
