# PROJECT BRIEF — YouTube Channel Built Around "Ancient Remedy Bible"

## 1. THE GOAL

Launch a YouTube channel that sells **"Ancient Remedy Bible"** — a ~45-remedy PDF ebook of natural home remedies (gut health, skin, sleep, stress, dental, bones/muscles/joints, hair, energy, nails, circulation, cold & flu). The videos are the funnel: each one demonstrates one remedy in the proven script format, then directs viewers to the book via a simple landing URL. The user has **real first-hand experience** in natural/home remedies — the persona is built on that, not fiction.

Deliverables: full video scripts in the exact style of the five reference scripts analyzed below, planned as a series (a rotating content wheel, not one-offs).

## 3. THE TEMPLATE — beat by beat (reusable for any topic)

This is the exact skeleton every script follows. Preserve the order:

1. **Hook** — a specific, relatable object/scene in the present tense ("There's a jug sitting in your kitchen…", "That rusty wrench in the coffee can…"). Sets up a surprising price or claim up front ($1,100 wrench, $1,100 skillet, $2 fence treatment).
2. **Persona intro** — name, place, authority, and years of experience stated plainly ("I'm Otis Granger, 40 years picking barns… I farm in Lancaster County, Pennsylvania.").
3. **Honest odds disclaimer** — undercuts hype, builds trust ("Most lures are $5–$10. Out of 100, maybe 1 worth over $100."). This is the "I'd rather you trust me than like me" beat.
4. **"Why old/basic beats new" educational primer** — 1–2 paragraphs explaining the underlying mechanic (before WWII tools were drop-forged thicker; scrap drives made survivors scarce; old foundries machined pan surfaces smooth). Establishes that knowledge is the product.
5. **Numbered list (5–10 items)** — each item contains:
   - The item + why people walk past it / throw it away
   - **The tell** (the stamp, eye, notch, seam, mark that separates $2 from $1,000) — always a physical, checkable detail
   - **A personal find story** (real-sounding, with a location and price: "at a sale in Pontiac, Illinois… $12, sold for $360")
   - **A 3-point checklist** ("Three things to check…")
   - Names of related brands/things to watch for
6. **"Hold up a second" book plug** (in the videos that have one) — placed mid-roll, right before the promised payoff item (#4 in a countdown), to hold retention. "The book is the long version." URL given. "I will not mention it again."
7. **Honest limitations / safety word** — a dedicated section ("I'll be square with you… it will not do X") + genuine safety warnings (rag fires, don't clean patina off, don't grind pans, know what the jug held).
8. **"Nobody teaches this" thesis** — the villain is not a conspiracy but "the business model" / "an information gap" / "nobody makes a living telling you the answer is already in your kitchen." Money runs the other way.
9. **Recap catchphrase** — one sticky line repeated: "Value hides in plain sight." / "The worth of a thing is in what it can still do."
10. **Comments CTA** — ask viewers their location or their own find ("drop a comment, tell me where you're watching from… I read every single one, I answer them too").
11. **Next-video teaser** — cross-serialization ("Next week: old cast iron… old outboard motors… a 30-year gate."). This is what makes it a channel, not one video.
12. **Closing line** — signature for the whole channel family: *"The people who built this country with their hands knew things we are only just starting to remember."*

## 4. STYLE / VOICE RULES (must match the reference scripts)

- Plain-spoken, folksy, direct. Second person ("you"). Contractions.
- Short, punchy sentences. Frequent "Now," / "Let me be straight with you" / "Here is the thing" transitions.
- Concrete numbers everywhere (prices, years, measurements). Never vague.
- Humble authority: modest about highs, honest about odds and limitations.
- **No emojis, no hype words, no affiliate-y language.** Trust is the product.
- Every claim about value/price is grounded in a plausible story with a named town/state.
- Safety beats are never skipped.

## 6. THE BOOK FUNNEL (selling the PDF)

- Pattern from the two scripts that did it (jug, lures): **one mid-roll plug** ("hold up a second," "the long version," URL) + **one outro mention**.
- **Our rule (improvement over the references): put the plug in EVERY video.** The reference set was inconsistent (2 of 5). Don't copy that.
- Landing page: simple, one URL (mirrors eliasodar.com / barnfindbible.com). **URL is TBD — needs to be decided/registered.**
- Framing: the book is "the long version" / "everything written down." Never pushy, always brief, then return to content.

## 7. HEALTH / YOUTUBE COMPLIANCE (critical — this is a medical-adjacent niche)

- **Demonstrate remedies, never make treatment claims.** Allowed: "supports sleep," "traditional practice says," "my grandmother did X," "reduces puffiness." **Banned: "cures," "treats," "fights infection," "heals,"** anything framed as medical advice.
- Every script must include an **honest limitations beat** ("this is not a substitute for [medicine/care], it's a home practice") and a **safety word** (allergies, contraindications, dosages, e.g., activated charcoal interacts with medication, don't give honey to infants, eggshell powder must be clean/sterile).
- Lead with the book's own disclaimer: "educational purposes only, not medical advice."
