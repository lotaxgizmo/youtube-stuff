Here is the exact method, step by step, the way I do it, and the way I would tell my own daughter. First, wash the area with plain soap and water, and dry it completely. Do not skip it. Third, soak your cotton pad or your cloth in the mixture, and press it onto the tag. Not the skin all around it, the tag itself. Leave it on for ten to fifteen minutes. I am going to say that again because it is the most common mistake in the world: ten to fifteen minutes, not an hour, not overnight, not until it stings. Ten to fifteen minutes.
