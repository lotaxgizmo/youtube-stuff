HOLISTIC REMEDIES FOR
BETTER SLEEP
SLEEP DOES NOT ABANDON US WITHOUT REASON. WHEN THE
BODY REFUSES REST, IT IS OFTEN BECAUSE SOMETHING WITHIN
REMAINS UNSETTLED, OVERSTIMULATED, OR OUT OF
BALANCE.
IN NATURAL RHYTHM, THE BODY FOLLOWS THE RISE AND FALL
OF LIGHT. AS DARKNESS COMES, IT PRODUCES MELATONIN, A
SIGNAL THAT QUIETS THE MIND AND PREPARES THE BODY FOR
SLEEP. YET IN THE MODERN WORLD, THIS RHYTHM IS EASILY
DISTURBED. BRIGHT ARTIFICIAL LIGHT, ESPECIALLY FROM
SCREENS, DELAYS THIS PROCESS AND CONFUSES THE BODY
INTO THINKING IT IS STILL DAY.
THE MIND ITSELF IS ANOTHER BARRIER. STRESS,
OVERTHINKING, AND UNRESOLVED THOUGHTS KEEP THE
NERVOUS SYSTEM ALERT, AS IF IT MUST REMAIN WATCHFUL.
EVEN WHEN THE BODY IS TIRED, THE MIND MAY CONTINUE TO
RACE, PREVENTING TRUE REST.
THE BODY CAN ALSO RESIST SLEEP WHEN IT IS PHYSICALLY
UNBALANCED. TOO MUCH CAFFEINE, IRREGULAR SLEEP TIMES,
HEAVY MEALS LATE AT NIGHT, OR LACK OF SUNLIGHT DURING
THE DAY CAN ALL INTERFERE WITH THE NATURAL SLEEP
CYCLE. IN OLDER TRADITIONS, THIS WAS OFTEN DESCRIBED AS
THE BODY BEING “OUT OF RHYTHM” WITH NATURE.
TRUE SLEEP COMES WHEN THE BODY FEELS SAFE, THE MIND
IS QUIET, AND THE INTERNAL CLOCK IS ALIGNED WITH
DARKNESS. THE REMEDIES THAT FOLLOW ARE NOT MEANT TO
FORCE SLEEP, BUT TO GENTLY GUIDE THE BODY BACK INTO ITS
NATURAL STATE OF REST.
